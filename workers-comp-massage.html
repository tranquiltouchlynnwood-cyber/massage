Workers' Comp & L&I Massage

Therapeutic massage for eligible Washington work-injury claims, focused on recovery and function within the authorized treatment plan.

Massage therapy for work-injury claims

Workers Injury / L&I Massage is for eligible Washington work-injury claims when applicable claim, referral and authorization requirements are met. Massage care is provided within the authorized treatment plan and focuses on the functional areas being addressed through massage therapy.

Information that may be needed

Clients should have their workers' compensation or L&I claim number, claim manager or payer information, attending provider information and any referral or authorization details required for treatment. Claim status and payer requirements can affect whether an appointment can be billed to the claim.

Coverage note: Contact Tranquil Touch before treatment to verify what information is needed for your claim.
