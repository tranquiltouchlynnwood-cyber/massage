MASSAGE THERAPY

Massage Therapy in Lynnwood

A customized 60-minute massage session that can incorporate multiple massage techniques based on your goals, comfort and therapist assessment.

Customized Massage Therapy

Massage Therapy at Tranquil Touch is a 60-minute appointment focused on muscular wellness, relaxation, mobility and the areas you would like addressed. Your therapist can adapt the session instead of requiring you to choose only one technique for the entire visit. The approach used depends on your goals, comfort, relevant history and what is appropriate within the scope of massage therapy.

Swedish Massage

Swedish Massage is a classic relaxation-focused style that commonly uses long gliding strokes, kneading and rhythmic techniques. It is well suited to clients who want a calming massage experience, general muscle relaxation and adjustable pressure throughout the session. Swedish techniques can also be blended with more focused work when specific areas need extra attention.

Deep Tissue Massage

Deep Tissue Massage uses slower and more concentrated techniques with firmer pressure to work on deeper muscular tension. The goal is not simply to make the massage intense; pressure is adjusted to your tolerance and the therapist works carefully through selected areas. Deep tissue techniques may be incorporated when you want focused attention to persistent tightness or overworked muscle groups.

Trigger Point Massage

Trigger Point Massage concentrates on specific areas of localized muscular tension and sensitivity. The therapist may apply sustained or focused pressure to selected points and surrounding tissue while monitoring your comfort. This approach is often blended with broader massage techniques rather than being used as a completely separate full-body style.

Stretch

Assisted Stretch can be incorporated into Massage Therapy to complement hands-on work and support comfortable mobility. Stretching may focus on areas that feel tight or restricted and is adjusted to your available range of motion. It is used as part of a customized session rather than as an aggressive flexibility program.

Percussive Therapy

Percussive Therapy uses a handheld percussion device to apply rapid, controlled pulses to selected muscle groups. When appropriate, it may be used before, during or after hands-on massage techniques to complement the session. The therapist selects the intensity and areas carefully and may avoid percussion where it is not appropriate.

Hot Stone Add-On

Hot Stone is an optional massage add-on using comfortably warmed stones to complement hands-on massage and relaxation. Stones may be used on selected areas during the appointment rather than replacing the massage itself. Because add-on pricing was not provided, the website directs clients to contact Tranquil Touch for current availability and pricing.

Cupping Add-On

Cupping is an optional enhancement that uses cups to create localized suction on selected areas. It can be incorporated alongside massage techniques when appropriate and requested. Clients interested in cupping should contact the practice before their appointment so availability, suitability and current pricing can be discussed.
