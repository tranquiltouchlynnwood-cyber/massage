Payment & Insurance

Self-pay pricing, accepted payment methods, medical insurance, Auto Injury/PIP and Workers Injury/L&I billing information.

Self-pay services

Massage Therapy, Head Spa and Facial appointments are each offered at $119 for 60 minutes when booked as self-pay wellness services.

Medical insurance

Tranquil Touch welcomes eligible insurance-based massage care. Coverage, network participation, referrals, authorization, deductibles, coinsurance, visit limits and claim requirements vary by plan and payer.

Auto Injury / PIP

Eligible auto-injury massage appointments may be billed to the applicable PIP claim when the required claim and billing information is available.

Workers Injury / L&I

Eligible Washington work-injury massage appointments may be billed to the applicable workers' compensation or L&I claim when claim and authorization requirements are satisfied.

Important: Insurance acceptance does not guarantee payment. Contact us before your appointment if you plan to use insurance or an injury claim.
