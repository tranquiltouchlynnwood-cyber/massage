AUTO INJURY / PIP

Auto Injury & PIP Massage

Therapeutic massage for eligible auto-injury claims, with claim information collected as needed for billing and documentation.

Massage therapy after an auto injury

Auto Injury / PIP Massage is intended for clients receiving massage therapy through an eligible automobile injury claim. These appointments are handled separately from standard self-pay wellness massage because the practice may need claim information, referral or prescription details, provider information and documentation needed for billing.

Information to have available

Before your first appointment, have your auto insurance carrier, claim number, adjuster information and any referral or prescription details available when applicable. Coverage, authorization, network status and claim requirements vary, so insurance acceptance does not guarantee payment for every visit.

Coverage note: Contact Tranquil Touch before treatment if you are unsure what your specific claim requires.
