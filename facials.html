Personalized facial care

The 60-minute Facial service provides a straightforward skin-care appointment focused on cleansing, hydration, skin renewal and relaxation. The provider can tailor the treatment to your skin and preferences while maintaining a calm, professional spa experience. Facials are booked as self-pay wellness appointments.

What to expect

Your appointment begins with a brief discussion of your skin and preferences, followed by cleansing and treatment steps selected for the service. Hydration and finishing care are incorporated before the appointment concludes. Clients with specific product sensitivities or skin concerns should discuss them with the provider before treatment.
