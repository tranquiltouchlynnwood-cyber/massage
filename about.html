About Tranquil Touch

A Lynnwood practice combining wellness services with eligible injury-focused massage care.

Clear services and professional care.

Tranquil Touch is organized around straightforward appointment types so clients can clearly understand what they are booking. Wellness services include Massage Therapy, Head Spa and Facials, while eligible injury-focused care includes Auto Injury / PIP Massage and Workers Injury / L&I Massage.

Massage Therapy may incorporate Swedish Massage, Deep Tissue, Trigger Point Massage, assisted Stretch and Percussive Therapy based on the client’s goals, comfort and therapist assessment. Hot Stone and Cupping are also offered as optional massage add-ons.

The practice is located in Lynnwood near Alderwood Mall and serves clients from Lynnwood and surrounding communities.
