Head Spa

A restorative 60-minute scalp-care experience with cleansing, relaxing scalp massage, hydration and finishing care.

A relaxing scalp-care experience

The Head Spa is a 60-minute wellness service centered on scalp cleansing, relaxing scalp massage, hydration and finishing care. It is designed for clients who want dedicated scalp attention in a calm spa environment. The appointment is separate from medical massage or injury-related billing and is offered as a self-pay service.

What the service includes

Your Head Spa appointment focuses on scalp-oriented care and relaxation. The provider can guide you through the treatment steps and adjust the experience based on your comfort and scalp-care needs. Because this is a wellness service, insurance billing information on the site applies to eligible massage therapy rather than the Head Spa.
